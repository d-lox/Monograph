# Monograph 🖋️✨
*A tactile, distraction-free retro typewriter app designed for short notes, essays, and deep writing.*

Monograph brings the addictive, mechanical feel of a physical typewriter to your mobile device. Built for writers and minimalists who crave sensory feedback, authentic typography, and absolute digital clutter-reduction.

## ✨ Core Features
* **POS Receipt Roll Feed:** A continuous vertical paper scroll that moves fluidly as you type and strike carriage returns.
* **Mechanical Sound & Haptic Engine:** Crisp, zero-latency keystroke audio feedback paired with tactile vibrations for every letter.
* **Night Light Mode:** An adjustable warm desk lamp spotlight illuminating your text for focused late-night writing sessions.
* **The Box (Archive):** Fully offline local storage (`localStorage`) to save, manage, and revisit all your past paper rolls.
* **Custom Toolbar & Settings:** Quick-access drawers for machine styling, lighting controls, and note archiving.

## 🛠️ Technical Architecture
* **Frontend:** High-performance, single-file HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript architecture.
* **Mobile Wrapper:** Powered by Capacitor for native Android deployment[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span).
